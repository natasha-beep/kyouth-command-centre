Training Missions & Exercises

Eight prepared hands-on missions with problem statements, data/materials, tasks, deliverables and facilitator guidance.

MISSION 01 · 21 Aug

Baseline Challenge: Can Management Trust the Portfolio View?

Three source tables covering companies, funding and milestones were maintained separately. Naming, statuses, ownership and definitions are inconsistent. Management wants one reliable view and three actions.

Mission 04: Automate the Business
