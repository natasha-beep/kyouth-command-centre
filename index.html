Programme Kick-off & Problem Framing

Open Full Day ↗

◎ Today’s Outcome

Understand the 37-day journey, set working standards and turn a vague operational pain point into a testable problem statement.

✓ Key Focus Areas

  • Write one manual-work symptom you have observed in any organisation.
  • Identify the stakeholder affected and the decision delayed by the problem.
  • Define the minimum data required to investigate it.
  • Write a one-sentence success criterion that could be tested at the end of a sprint.
