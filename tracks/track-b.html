Partner Follow-up Automation

Partner follow-up can become fragmented across notes, messages and spreadsheets. Build a controlled follow-up queue with owner, next action, due date, priority and escalation logic; automate a management digest without inventing partner facts.

Prepared project mission

Partner follow-up can become fragmented across notes, messages and spreadsheets. Build a controlled follow-up queue with owner, next action, due date, priority and escalation logic; automate a management digest without inventing partner facts.

Required build

  • Trusted input transformation and exception handling.
  • At least one automated repeatable step.
  • Management-facing interface/report with next action.
  • Failure/exception evidence and runbook.
  • Sponsor validation against explicit acceptance criteria.
