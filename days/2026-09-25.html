Trainer / facilitator control notes

Facilitation posture: demonstrate only enough to unblock the next decision. Do not take over the keyboard. Ask for evidence at every checkpoint.

  • Check that the graduate can explain why the chosen tool/pattern fits the problem.
  • Reject outputs that hide unresolved exceptions or unverifiable numbers.
  • Require a working artifact or evidence before marking the day complete.
  • For public GitHub work, use synthetic/sanitised data only.

⚙ Exercise / Activity

From Programme to Operating Habit

The final day consolidates what was built, who owns it and what the graduate should deliver in the first month after the structured programme.

  1. Complete project handover and repository/file transfer.
  2. Compare baseline evidence with final capability.
  3. Write one technical strength and one development priority.
  4. Agree one 30-day mission with the division sponsor.
  5. Define check-in cadence and success evidence.
  6. Close with a two-minute reflection: problem → build → automate → impact.
