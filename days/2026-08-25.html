▣ What You Will Learn
Frame the business decision before selecting a tool.
Produce tangible evidence rather than passive attendance.
Make assumptions, validation rules and failure paths visible.
Explain what management/user should do next.
◎ MRANTI Impact
Converts trusted data into a management product rather than a chart collection.
Trainer / facilitator control notes

Facilitation posture: demonstrate only enough to unblock the next decision. Do not take over the keyboard. Ask for evidence at every checkpoint.

  • Check that the graduate can explain why the chosen tool/pattern fits the problem.
  • Reject outputs that hide unresolved exceptions or unverifiable numbers.
  • Require a working artifact or evidence before marking the day complete.
  • For public GitHub work, use synthetic/sanitised data only.

⚙ Exercise / Activity

Mission 02: Portfolio Performance Control Room

The data layer is trusted, but management still needs a fast answer to: where are the risks, which records require intervention, and what should happen next?

  1. Build the company-to-funding and company-to-milestone model.
  2. Create the required DAX measures from the build guide.
  3. Design a KPI strip that distinguishes trusted totals from exceptions.
  4. Build an overdue milestone risk table with owner and days overdue.
  5. Show approved vs disbursed funding by a meaningful business dimension.
  6. Write three prioritised management actions and test whether a director can understand them in 20 seconds.
Required deliverables
One-page Power BI report
Validated KPI measures
Risk table
Exception panel
Three management actions
Screenshot/export for review
