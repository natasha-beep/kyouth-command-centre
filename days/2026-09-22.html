Trainer / facilitator control notes

Facilitation posture: demonstrate only enough to unblock the next decision. Do not take over the keyboard. Ask for evidence at every checkpoint.

  • Check that the graduate can explain why the chosen tool/pattern fits the problem.
  • Reject outputs that hide unresolved exceptions or unverifiable numbers.
  • Require a working artifact or evidence before marking the day complete.
  • For public GitHub work, use synthetic/sanitised data only.

⚙ Exercise / Activity

Silent User Test

The user gets a short task and must use the product while the graduate observes silently. The goal is to find confusion, not defend the design.

  1. Give the user one realistic decision/task.
  2. Observe without explaining the interface.
  3. Record where the user hesitates, misreads or asks for help.
  4. Ask what action they would take based on the output.
  5. Capture pass/fail against relevant acceptance criteria.
  6. Prioritise fixes that change correctness or decision clarity.
