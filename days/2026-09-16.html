◎ MRANTI Impact

Trainer / facilitator control notes

Facilitation posture: demonstrate only enough to unblock the next decision. Do not take over the keyboard. Ask for evidence at every checkpoint.

  • Check that the graduate can explain why the chosen tool/pattern fits the problem.
  • Reject outputs that hide unresolved exceptions or unverifiable numbers.
  • Require a working artifact or evidence before marking the day complete.
  • For public GitHub work, use synthetic/sanitised data only.

⚙ Exercise / Activity

30-Minute Sponsor Review

The graduate shows the problem, live prototype, one exception and one next action. Feedback is recorded as decisions, defects or scope changes.

  1. Restate the sponsor decision and agreed scope.
  2. Demo the working flow from input to management output.
  3. Show one failure/exception path.
  4. Walk through acceptance criteria and mark pass/partial/fail.
  5. Capture every request as decision, defect, enhancement or out-of-scope.
  6. Agree the top three changes for the next sprint.
