Trainer / facilitator control notes

Facilitation posture: demonstrate only enough to unblock the next decision. Do not take over the keyboard. Ask for evidence at every checkpoint.

  • Check that the graduate can explain why the chosen tool/pattern fits the problem.
  • Reject outputs that hide unresolved exceptions or unverifiable numbers.
  • Require a working artifact or evidence before marking the day complete.
  • For public GitHub work, use synthetic/sanitised data only.

⚙ Exercise / Activity

AI QA Challenge: Find, Explain, Verify

Graduates receive a trusted KPI payload and exception list. They must ask AI to summarise issues and recommended actions while preventing invented IDs, unsupported totals or hidden assumptions.

  1. Write a context-rich prompt that separates verified facts from interpretation.
  2. Require a fixed JSON structure for summary, critical issues, overdue items and actions.
  3. Create a validation checklist: allowed IDs, allowed totals, required keys, no unsupported claims.
  4. Run at least two adversarial prompt tests.
  5. Document one case where AI output sounded plausible but failed verification.
