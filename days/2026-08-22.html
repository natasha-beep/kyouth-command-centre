Trainer / facilitator control notes

Facilitation posture: demonstrate only enough to unblock the next decision. Do not take over the keyboard. Ask for evidence at every checkpoint.

  • Check that the graduate can explain why the chosen tool/pattern fits the problem.
  • Reject outputs that hide unresolved exceptions or unverifiable numbers.
  • Require a working artifact or evidence before marking the day complete.
  • For public GitHub work, use synthetic/sanitised data only.

⚙ Exercise / Activity

MRANTI-Style Event Registration Clean-up Challenge

Event registration, participant, payment and feedback records arrive from different channels. A coordinator repeatedly cleans dates, statuses and emails, merges payment information and manually finds follow-up requests.

  1. Connect to all four CSV sources.
  2. Trim and standardise text/date fields.
  3. Flag duplicate registrations instead of silently deleting them.
  4. Merge participant and payment data using a controlled key.
  5. Create `needs_follow_up` from feedback and payment status.
  6. Output one clean attendee table and one exception table, then refresh it from source.
