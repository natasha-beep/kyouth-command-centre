# Track D — Utilities & Facilities Exception Monitor

## Prepared problem statement
Utility and facility usage records require repeated comparison to find abnormal cost/usage movements. Build an exception monitor that identifies material changes, records investigation ownership and produces a monthly action queue.

## Dataset
`resources/data/division_tracks/track_D_utility_monitor.csv`

## Required solution
- Reproducible input transformation.
- Clear decision rules and exceptions.
- At least one automated step.
- Management-facing interface/report.
- README / operating notes.
- Demonstrated failure or exception path.

## Business-value test
Show which manual step is removed, what risk becomes visible, and what management can act on faster.
