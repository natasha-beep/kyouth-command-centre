# 5-Minute Management Pitch Guide

**Minute 0–1 — Decision:** What business decision is this product helping management make?

**Minute 1–2 — Trust:** What data is used, what was reconciled, and what remains unresolved?

**Minute 2–3 — Working product:** Show the live output or automation. Do not narrate slides when a live artifact exists.

**Minute 3–4 — Value:** State the most important insight, time saved, risk reduced or decision improved.

**Minute 4–5 — Control:** Explain failure handling, limitations, owner and next action.
