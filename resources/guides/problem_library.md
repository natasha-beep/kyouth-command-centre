# MRANTI-Relevant Synthetic Problem Library

These are training scenarios, not claims about current MRANTI production systems.

## 1. Portfolio reporting reconciliation
Different owners maintain company, funding and milestone records with inconsistent naming and statuses. Management needs one trusted view and visible exceptions.

## 2. Recurring data refresh
New records arrive each reporting cycle. Manual cleaning creates dependency on one analyst and makes duplicate loads or silent failures possible.

## 3. Management exception update
An analyst manually checks overdue milestones and funding exceptions, writes a summary and sends follow-up. Build an auditable automation with deterministic checks and controlled AI summarisation.

## 4. Submission / compliance checker
Files and evidence are reviewed manually. Build a structured first-pass checker that distinguishes complete, partial, missing and unable-to-verify evidence.

## 5. Partner follow-up operations
Partner outreach is tracked across messages and spreadsheets. Build a follow-up queue with next action, owner, due date and escalation logic.

## 6. Commercialisation pipeline control
Opportunity stages, next actions and evidence live in fragmented records. Build one management view that makes stalled opportunities and missing ownership visible.

## 7. Utilities / facilities exception monitoring
Monthly usage and cost records are manually compared. Build an exception view that flags abnormal changes and records investigation status.
