# Track B — Partner Follow-up Automation

## Prepared problem statement
Partner follow-up can become fragmented across notes, messages and spreadsheets. Build a controlled follow-up queue with owner, next action, due date, priority and escalation logic; automate a management digest without inventing partner facts.

## Dataset
`resources/data/division_tracks/track_B_partner_followup.csv`

## Required solution
- Reproducible input transformation.
- Clear decision rules and exceptions.
- At least one automated step.
- Management-facing interface/report.
- README / operating notes.
- Demonstrated failure or exception path.

## Business-value test
Show which manual step is removed, what risk becomes visible, and what management can act on faster.
