# K-Youth Assessment Rubric

| Area | Weight | Full-mark behaviour |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem framing | 20 | Clarifies decision, stakeholder, grain, definitions and assumptions before building. |
| Data quality / handling | 20 | Finds duplicates, unmatched records, inconsistent labels and date logic; reconciles totals; no silent deletion. |
| Technical approach | 20 | Appropriate architecture, clean logic, reusable components, clear trade-offs. |
| Automation / reliability | 15 | Repeatable flow, idempotent behaviour, logging, visible failure path, appropriate recovery. |
| Insight / communication | 10 | States what happened, why it matters and what action is required. |
| Documentation | 10 | README/runbook, assumptions, lineage/mapping, limitations and operating notes are complete. |
| Independence / judgement | 5 | Uses AI/tools actively, verifies outputs and makes sound decisions. |
